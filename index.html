About us

Built on decades of front-line sales & service experience

Samurai HealthCare Private Limited was founded by a team with more than 25 years of experience in the Operation Theatre Surgical Light, Surgical Table and C-Arm business. The team worked as front-line, hard-core sales and service professionals with brands such as Steris, Allen Spine & Stirrups, Berchtold, Trumpf, BenQ and Ziehm C-Arms in India.

With that depth of sales and service experience and technical skill, Samurai HealthCare took responsibility for the Dr. Mach (Germany) surgical lights business. Since then we have added specialised surgical products, including surgical tables from AMTAI, USA, carbon fibre spine frame accessories, and premium accessories for a wide range of surgical procedures.

Our team can also provide service support for operation theatre lights, operation theatre tables and related equipment from any leading imported brand.
